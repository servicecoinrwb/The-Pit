Seeding mints locked shares to a burn address, putting a permanent floor under the share supply — until it runs, an unseeded vault is exactly the state a first-depositor attack needs. Seeded assets can never be withdrawn by anyone. A thousand is plenty; to fund the pool, deposit on the portfolio view instead.

Swapping the desk is the most dangerous action here, because the desk can move money out of the pool. The first wiring is immediate; every change after waits 48 hours so depositors who disagree can withdraw first, and it refuses outright while capital is reserved against open positions.
